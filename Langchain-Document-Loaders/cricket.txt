The Symphony of Willow and Leather

Beneath the sun, the field awakes,
A hush descends, no sound it makes.
The bowler strides with measured pace,
Eyes locked upon the striker’s face.

The ball released, it cuts the air,
A spinning thread, a subtle snare.
The willow sings, a crack resounds,
Echoing joy through grassy grounds.

The fielder dives, the crowd takes breath,
A dance with chance, with life and death.
Each run a story, bold and bright,
Each wicket lost, a fading light.

Yet in this game, beyond the score,
Lives passion, pride, and something more.
For cricket’s heart beats fierce and true,
A timeless bond for me and you.
